THERMAL PREDICTION MODEL EVALUATION
==================================

Dataset:
NASA lithium-ion battery discharge measurements

Prediction task:
Predict the next observed battery temperature.

Evaluation:
Battery-level train/test split.

Best model by test MAE:
Random Forest

MAE:
0.04608 °C

RMSE:
0.10077 °C

R²:
0.99996

Models compared:
               Model   MAE_C  RMSE_C      R2
Persistence Baseline 0.11851 0.17634 0.99988
   Linear Regression 0.06779 0.10362 0.99996
       Random Forest 0.04608 0.10077 0.99996

Limitations:
1. Results apply to the selected held-out batteries and split.
2. Next-observation prediction is not fixed-horizon forecasting.
3. Performance should be compared with persistence.
4. Physical parameters require independent justification or calibration.
5. The physics-regularized prototype is not a fully validated electrothermal PINN.
